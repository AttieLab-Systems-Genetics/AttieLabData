## GeDI Community Discussion

Dozens of studies in Diversity Outbred, Collaborative Cross, and CC-RIX mice hold rich genetic, molecular, and phenotypic data, but much of it is hard to find, combine, or reuse. This workshop invites the CTC community to discuss how existing GeDi studies can be curated into shared, AI-ready resources while data contributors retain ownership, receive credit, and set the terms for access and use.

We will briefly introduce the
[GeDi Data Ecosystem](https://www.jax.org/research-and-faculty/genetic-diversity-initiative),
a JAX effort to harmonize legacy studies and make them accessible to AI agent-based analysis, and then open the floor to questions of governance, attribution, and what the community would want from such a resource.
